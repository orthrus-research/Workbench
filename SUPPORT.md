# Workbench support

Workbench support is organized through
[`orthrus-research/workbench`](https://github.com/orthrus-research/workbench).

## Where to ask

- Use a
  [bug report](https://github.com/orthrus-research/workbench/issues/new?template=bug.yml)
  for reproducible Workbench failures.
- Use a
  [feature request](https://github.com/orthrus-research/workbench/issues/new?template=feature.yml)
  for a developer problem or capability that Workbench could address.
- Read the [documentation index](docs/README.md), the
  [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md), and existing issues before opening a
  new issue.

Public issue creation is enabled. `zestehl` is the responsible maintainer.
Participation follows the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), which provides
a private route for conduct reports.

## What to include

Name the Workbench component and exact revision or version, host platform,
Cleanroom or pack profile, smallest reproduction, expected behavior, and
actual behavior. Include text logs when they are safe to share. Replace
usernames and local paths, and do not upload credentials, personal worlds,
licensed game or mod artifacts, or private source.

Support is bounded by the published component version, compatibility record,
and support matrix. Development snapshots before the first release have no
public support commitment. Supersymmetry is the first pack profile; behavior
observed there is not automatically a universal rule for every Minecraft
1.12.2 pack. Experimental capabilities are not stable support commitments.

## Requests that need a different route

- Report suspected vulnerabilities through the private process in
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not in an issue.
- Questions about Minecraft accounts, launchers, mod redistribution, or
  third-party services belong with their respective providers.
- General mod-development discussion without a concrete Workbench problem is
  outside the issue tracker's scope.

Send conduct reports privately to [zestehl@pm.me](mailto:zestehl@pm.me), as
described in the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Do not send them through
a public issue or the vulnerability-reporting route.
